The studio

Two names,

Hanin & Hala is a beauty studio on Al-Faqqasa Roundabout in Tartus. We work on nails, brows, lashes and feet — slowly, precisely, and with a finish that lasts.

The philosophy is simple: the result does the talking. Not the signage, not the promises — just the detail you can see for yourself.
